# Grill Me

Use only when the user explicitly asks to be grilled, challenged, or interviewed, or when a major design decision cannot be made safely without a structured interrogation.

Read existing plans and working documents first. Ask one specific question at a time, include a recommendation, and avoid questions already answered by repository facts. Follow dependencies from assumptions and terminology through data, behavior, UI, persistence, tests, and rollout.

End with settled decisions, open decisions, risks/contradictions, and the smallest next artifact or action.
