# Topic 07 — Taste Quality Index comparison

Approved 05 direction keeps structure, taste fit, style, typicity and faults distinct; the scoring formula and Taste Quality Index’s role remain open ([`docs/WorkingDocs/design.md`](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md), decisions 31 and 39, open question at line 124). Migration rows 141 and 143 say no inspected sibling provides wine-family taste evaluation; they call for a shared moving-target framework and explicit separation of sensory assessment from commercial value ([`docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_System_Migration_Research.md`](../../docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_System_Migration_Research.md)).

The closest useful precedent is not a taste analogue. Office Tycoon’s `calculateSoftwareQuality` combines architecture quality with averaged feature scores (`C:/GitHub/officetycoon/src/engine/product/developmentEngine.ts`); its release engine validates that quality but records chosen and base prices separately (`C:/GitHub/officetycoon/src/engine/product/releaseEngine.ts`). Industri Clicker exposes its scalar input ceiling, research/upgrade/production/staff limits, bonus and multiplier as `OutputQualityBreakdown` (`C:/GitHub/industriClicker/game/quality/qualityMath.ts`). Its offer-generation path snapshots inventory quality into the order reward; fulfillment pays that reward while separately recording delivered inventory quality (`C:/GitHub/industriClicker/game/sales/salesOrders.ts`, `C:/GitHub/industriClicker/game/core/stores/gameStore.ts`). These support traceable breakdowns and separating current assessment, historical offer terms and price—not a 05 wine formula.

05 currently declares separate structure/taste `ProfileAssessment`s, a quality scalar, faults and descriptors in [`src/game/evaluation/types.ts`](../../src/game/evaluation/types.ts), and a generic weighted evaluator with per-axis fits/reasons in [`src/game/evaluation/MovingTargets.ts`](../../src/game/evaluation/MovingTargets.ts). `Sensory.ts` derives only structure. `CompanyView.ts` imports `assessWine`, but no implementation file exists at this inspection. Bottling already retains a separate assessment snapshot (`src/game/wine/types.ts`; [`tests/game/wine-operations.test.ts`](../../tests/game/wine-operations.test.ts)).

Recommended: assess current structure and taste profiles independently, report their axis breakdowns and physical faults separately, and let commercial pricing consume assessment through its own explainable breakdown. Keep historical snapshots distinct from current readings; do not hard-code 04’s 50/50 average. Key existing checks are [`tests/game/moving-targets.test.ts`](../../tests/game/moving-targets.test.ts), [`tests/game/sensory.test.ts`](../../tests/game/sensory.test.ts), Office `tests/developmentEngine.test.ts` / `tests/releaseEngine.test.ts`, and Industri `tests/sales/salesOrders.test.ts` (locks offer quality into reward). Tests were not run for this research.
