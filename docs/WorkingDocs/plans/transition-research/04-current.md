# Topic 04 — Six structural characteristics

## Current 05 boundary

The 15 November decisions require current sensory profiles to derive from current wine state and keep structural balance separate from taste fit; the exact axes and formulas remain open ([design.md](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md), decisions 31, 38–39, and open questions). The active contract proposes pure `deriveStructure(lot)` with no score inputs ([transition-contracts.md](../transition-contracts.md)). Migration research retains contextual moving targets but leaves axes open ([row 140](../../docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_System_Migration_Research.md#L140)); architecture places sensory projection after material development ([wine interface](../../docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker05_Technical_Architecture_Proposal.md#L324)). In source, [wine/types.ts](../../src/game/wine/types.ts) separates immutable `RawMaterialAnchors` from mutable `PhysicalState`; [evaluation/types.ts](../../src/game/evaluation/types.ts) declares six axes. No `evaluation/Sensory.ts` exists. Anchors include sugar, acidity, phenolics, aromatics, body and aging potential, but no spice potential. `PhysicalState` has acidity, aroma, phenolics, sugar, alcohol, oak, lees, extraction-related and risk state, but no body, sweetness, spice or tannin output fields.

## 04 evidence and limits

I inspected the local Winemaker04 checkout at HEAD `94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26`; the transition overview cites an older revision. Its [WineCharacteristics type](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/types/types.ts#L108) defines acidity, aroma, body, spice, sweetness and tannins on 0–1 scales. Harvest construction in [inventoryService.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/winery/inventoryService.ts#L171) applies direct characteristic deltas for ripeness, land-value modifier, altitude, suitability, overgrowth and density ([harvestCharacteristics.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/characteristics/harvestCharacteristics.ts#L51)). Crushing again directly nudges traits, while increasing pressing yield and reducing aroma/spice as tannins rise ([crushingCharacteristics.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/characteristics/crushingCharacteristics.ts#L96)). Fermentation adds fixed weekly deltas by method and temperature, clamped to 0–1 ([fermentationCharacteristics.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/characteristics/fermentationCharacteristics.ts#L110)). Taste families then reuse characteristics plus anchors, grape identity and feature severity ([wineTasteProfileService.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/taste/wineTasteProfileService.ts#L49)); anchors also shift structural targets ([wineAnchorCharacteristicBridge.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/anchors/wineAnchorCharacteristicBridge.ts#L117)). These are game abstractions, not validated chemistry, and the same inputs reach multiple evaluations. Treat the trade-offs as scenarios, not formulas to port.

## Proposed projection and checks

Derive the six-axis profile as a read-only query after physical transformations: acidity and aroma from current material; sweetness from residual sugar relative to harvest sugar; tannins from expressed phenolics against remaining capacity; body from current alcohol/extract/lees shaped by body potential; spice from oak, extraction and maturation. Use raw potential as a baseline or response capacity, not a second additive copy of the same present material. If varietal spice should exist without oak/process, decide whether to add an explicit inherited field or use grape identity. Normalize each output into finite 0–1 values with named input mappings. Do not read or mutate structure/taste/quality scores, targets, prices or persisted traits.

Proposed deterministic tests (not run): repeated calls preserve the lot and return identical bounded axes; harvest ripeness changes physical sugar/acidity before projection; higher pressing yield trades against aromatic retention and extracted phenolics; fermentation sugar depletion changes sweetness while skin contact changes tannin/body; oak/maturation and bottle development change current projection without rewriting a bottling snapshot. Winemaker04 has unit tests for fixed fermentation deltas, but I found no matching harvest/crush projection tests; this was source inspection only.
