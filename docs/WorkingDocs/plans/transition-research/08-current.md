# Topic08 — Bounded contextual target profiles

Approved direction keeps structural and taste moving targets, with most structural synergies and penalties absorbed into targets and physical faults separate ([design.md](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md#L12)). The active contract assigns `structureTargets(lot: PhysicalLot, profile: StructureProfile): TargetProfile` and `tasteTargets(lot: PhysicalLot, profile: TasteProfile): TargetProfile`; targets carry reasons and add no second synergy penalty ([transition-contracts.md](../transition-contracts.md#L56)). Current [evaluation types](../../src/game/evaluation/types.ts) define six structure axes, eight fixed prototype taste families, bounded target fields, and separate `WineFault`; [MovingTargets.ts](../../src/game/evaluation/MovingTargets.ts) validates exact axis coverage and normalized ranges, then evaluates one weighted fit. `Profiles.ts` is absent at inspection. [PhysicalLot](../../src/game/wine/types.ts) carries per-source grape identity, anchors, and fraction; [GrapeDefinition](../../src/game/vineyard/types.ts) provides color and inherited potentials. These support source-weighted contexts for blends; do not import 04’s 14 taste-family count.

At Winemaker04 HEAD `94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26`, `C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/wineStructure/calculations/rangeCalculator.ts` shifts ranges sequentially from current characteristics, clamps endpoints and minimum width, but has no total shift budget. `C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/wineStructure/calculations/ruleCalculator.ts` then separately multiplies penalties and applies synergy reductions; `C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/wineStructure/config/rules.ts` includes high-tannin/low-body penalty and high-acidity/high-tannin synergy. `C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/services/wine/anchors/wineAnchorCharacteristicBridge.ts` adds anchor-based midpoint and width changes. 04 taste targets offer a useful pattern—grape/color bases, profile dependencies, and reasons—in `C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/services/wine/taste/tasteQualityIndexService.ts`; its taxonomy and values are not 05 decisions.

**Proposed take:** Build deterministic per-source base contexts from grape identity and harvest anchors, combine by source fractions, then apply only a small named set of one-way cross-axis adjustments from the original profile. Cap total movement and target width; never shift an axis from its own observed value or feed adjusted targets back into more rules. Record source and interaction reasons on each axis. Let `evaluateProfile` score once; keep physical-fault diagnosis in `WineFault`, outside target construction. Exact axes-to-context weights and shift limits remain open.

Critical scenarios: same profile under different grape sources yields deterministic, distinct targets; a blend reflects source fractions; high tannins with low body can leave body underfit, while extreme tannins cannot widen their own target; a permitted cross-axis shift applies once and explains why; changing targets changes fit only, not `deriveStructure` or fault findings; eight taste families remain independent from structure and faults.
