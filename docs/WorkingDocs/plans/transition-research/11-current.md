# Topic 11 — Maturation and vessel integration

**05 direction:** Approved decisions 26–31 in [design.md](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md) assign physical wine state to wine, amounts and placement to inventory, and vessel properties/condition to equipment. Lots in different environments develop separately; one entry point advances fermentation, extraction, maturation, risks and bottle evolution. Current cellar assessments/outlooks are derived while the bottling assessment stays historical.

At this inspection, [equipment/types.ts](../../src/game/equipment/types.ts) has steel/oak/concrete, capacity, condition and uses; [wine/types.ts](../../src/game/wine/types.ts) separates anchors, physical state, process settings, form and environment; [inventory/Rules.ts](../../src/game/inventory/Rules.ts) prevents independent liquid states sharing a vessel. [transition-contracts.md](../transition-contracts.md) specifies `resolveWineEnvironment`, `advanceMaturation`, `developLot` and derived `maturityOutlook`, including provisional oxygen-ingress baselines (steel 0.0008, oak 0.006, concrete 0.003, bottle 0.0002 per day before headspace/condition effects); maturation may exceed 1 to support decline. `equipment/Rules.ts`, `wine/Maturation.ts` and `wine/Development.ts` were absent from this checkout, although [CompanyView.ts](../../src/game/queries/CompanyView.ts) and [AdvanceTime.ts](../../src/game/simulation/AdvanceTime.ts) already import them: these causal rules are contracted, not yet implemented here.

**04 evidence (checkout pinned at `94fd950f`):** [storageVesselAllocationService.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/winery/storageVesselAllocationService.ts) and [storageVesselUseLedger migration](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/migrations/archive/20260721200000_storage_vessel_use_ledger_rollout.sql) enforce capacity/allocation, record filled litres, and increment fill history/dirty status on first use; condition is stored and maintained separately. The one-plan/one-vessel constraint is in [20260722090000_enforce_one_vessel_per_wine_batch.sql](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/migrations/20260722090000_enforce_one_vessel_per_wine_batch.sql). However, [storageVesselExposureService.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/winery/storageVesselExposureService.ts) only defines capacity-contact and condition/fill-history expression calculations; neither has a source caller. Bottle aging is connected: [gameTick.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/core/gameTick.ts) increments age for stocked bottled wine; [featureService.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/features/featureService.ts) consumes [bottleAging.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/features/constants/bottleAging.ts) for feature-based characteristic/quality changes, and [wineScoreCalculation.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/winescore/wineScoreCalculation.ts) applies the configured market-price premium. Severity plateaus; it is bottle-only, not a causal bulk/bottle curve with decline.
