# Topic 11 comparison: maturation and vessels

Approved decisions 26-31 in [design.md](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md) put physical development on wine lots, quantity/location/occupancy in inventory, and vessel properties/condition in equipment. [Transition contracts](../transition-contracts.md) define a one-lot vessel rule and `WineEnvironment` inputs in actual Celsius plus normalized daily oxygen ingress, oak strength and contamination pressure. Provisional ingress baselines are steel 0.0008, oak 0.006, concrete 0.003 and bottle 0.0002 per day before fill and condition effects. [wine/types.ts](../../src/game/wine/types.ts) includes `ageDays`, maturation, oak, lees and an immutable bottling assessment; [equipment/types.ts](../../src/game/equipment/types.ts) currently stores material, capacity, condition and use count, not detailed fill history. [inventory/Rules.ts](../../src/game/inventory/Rules.ts) rejects another live lot and compares volume with supplied free capacity.

The pinned [migration inventory](../../docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_System_Migration_Research.md) identifies WhiskeyMixer's [cask assignment](https://github.com/gram12321/WhiskeyMixer/blob/8cebe03fbe2ed19715a3825d50c19119c14d58f6/src/store/gameStore.ts#L160), [weekly tick](https://github.com/gram12321/WhiskeyMixer/blob/8cebe03fbe2ed19715a3825d50c19119c14d58f6/src/store/gameStore.ts#L71) and [age arithmetic](https://github.com/gram12321/WhiskeyMixer/blob/8cebe03fbe2ed19715a3825d50c19119c14d58f6/src/utils/time.ts#L40). Casks store type, capacity and condition; assignment only requires an empty cask, while batch quality is recalculated from base quality and age and cask condition decays independently. Quality improves to about week 130, plateaus to 260, then declines to half-base floor, while market price retains a logarithmic age bonus. The calendar advances 48 weeks/year but age arithmetic counts 52, causing a five-week jump at rollover. Do not port cask quality or age pricing. Industri's [facility condition](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/facilities/facility.ts#L165) affects operational efficiency and supports repair; VisualIdle's [incoming transport accounting](https://github.com/gram12321/visualidle/blob/8d186331e838ab49c82fb11d9452ba09a9e0b893/src/game/storage/WarehouseStorageRules.ts#L125) tracks cargo committed to a destination. These are maintenance/capacity analogies, not wine rules.

05 should derive causal exposure from material and its fill history, occupied/capacity headspace, condition, actual cellar temperature, lees contact and time; keep bulk and bottle evolution in the same pure daily `developLot` path. Derive improvement, maturity window, decline and approximate forecast from current state and aging potential; preserve the bottling snapshot and avoid a scalar age bonus.

Critical scenarios: compare steel/oak/concrete and fresh/reused oak at matched lot, fill, temperature and duration; vary headspace, condition, lees and cellar temperature independently; verify no age jump at year rollover and improvement-to-window-to-decline; confirm bulk-to-bottle continuity with the historical snapshot unchanged; test transfer/split occupancy and capacity invariants; and assert repeated forecasts are deterministic and leave live state untouched.
