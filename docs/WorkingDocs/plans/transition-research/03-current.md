# 03 — Current research: Terroir Potential, Origin Prestige, and land value

## 05 status and evidence

The approved direction replaces 04’s single Land Value Modifier with agricultural **Terroir Potential** and commercial **Origin Prestige**; formulas and their relationship to vineyard/company prestige remain open ([design.md](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md), decisions 10 and 40). The contract names `vineyard/Origin.ts` with `terroirPotential(site, grape, condition)`, independent `originPrestige(site)`, and a land-valuation query ([transition-contracts.md](../transition-contracts.md)). No 05 `Origin.ts` or vineyard catalog exists. Current [vineyard types](../../src/game/vineyard/types.ts) provide drainage, sunlight, fertility, altitude, prestige and price per hectare, but grape data has no environmental preferences; [WineOrigin](../../src/game/wine/types.ts) snapshots both outputs at harvest.

## What the 04 source demonstrates

04’s `calculateLandValueModifier` combines normalized land price (60%) and bounded vineyard prestige (40%), then multiplies by overgrowth and density factors (`C:\GitHub\winemaker04\src\lib\services\wine\winescore\landValueModifierCalculation.ts:96`). Harvest anchors use that modifier with grape suitability, regional fit, soil, sunlight and health (`C:\GitHub\winemaker04\src\lib\services\wine\anchors\wineAnchorService.ts:103`). The same modifier drives a 0.2–3.0 wine-price multiplier, while estimated price separately multiplies company and vineyard prestige (`C:\GitHub\winemaker04\src\lib\services\wine\winescore\wineScoreCalculation.ts:32`). Land valuation also has another path: `calculateLandValue` uses region, altitude and aspect; `calculateAdjustedLandValue` adds grape suitability, vine age and vineyard prestige (`C:\GitHub\winemaker04\src\lib\services\vineyard\vineyardValueCalc.ts:61`, `:350`). These overlapping inputs make expensive ground, suitability and reputation reinforce each other in crop quality and price. Finance also applies the wine land multiplier to grape inventory (`C:\GitHub\winemaker04\src\lib\services\finance\financeService.ts:334`). The transition overview describes 04 observations, not a 05 specification (§§2.3, 2.12).

## Recommended independent calculations

1. **Terroir Potential (0–1):** calculate grape-specific environmental fit from drainage, sunlight, fertility and altitude, with response curves authored per grape; use weights `0.30, 0.30, 0.20, 0.20`. Then apply a bounded condition factor: `fit × (0.80 + 0.20 × clamp(condition,0,1))`. Do not read site prestige or price. The necessary grape response data is absent today, so add it to the catalog before implementing this formula; do not treat grape sugar/aroma traits as site suitability. Snapshot the result on harvested origin and consume it only in raw-material anchors/harvest expression.
2. **Origin Prestige (0–1):** use the authored `SiteDefinition.prestige`, clamped to range, as the static site reputation. Do not derive it from land cost, agricultural fit, condition or current vineyard/company prestige. Let a commercial quote apply it once as `1 + 0.25 × originPrestige` (0–25% premium); retain a distinct company-brand factor only if desired, and do not also multiply dynamic vineyard prestige until it has a separately defined, non-duplicative role.
3. **Land value:** return `round(site.pricePerHaCents × areaHa)` as the asset valuation. Do not adjust it by grape fit, condition, prestige or wine price. Author `pricePerHaCents` independently from `prestige`; otherwise high site cost can produce both asset-based company reputation and an Origin Prestige wine premium from the same input.

For an illustrative fixture only, fits `.8/.9/.6/.7` yield site fit `.77`; condition `.8` gives Terroir Potential `.7392`. Prestige `.68` gives a 1.17 quote multiplier. At 420,000 cents/ha over 2.5 ha, land value is 1,050,000 cents. Changing one result’s inputs must leave the other two outputs unchanged.

## Verification recommendations

Add focused `Origin.ts` tests: deterministic/clamped outputs; suitability or condition changes potential only; site prestige changes prestige and quote once; and area or `pricePerHaCents` changes asset value only. Add a harvest scenario proving stored `WineOrigin` values stay fixed after later condition/reputation changes. Existing 04 comparison suites include `tests/vineyard/grapeSuitability.test.ts`, `tests/wine/wineAnchorService.test.ts` and `tests/prestige/prestigeService.test.ts`; their formulas are not 05 acceptance criteria. No tests were run: this was source research only.
