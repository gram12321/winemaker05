# 01 — Vineyard characteristics beyond Winemaker04

## Evidence from 05

The approved 15 November design separates **Terroir Potential** (agricultural) from **Origin Prestige** (commercial). It calls for retaining inherited raw-material potential as a harvest baseline, while tracking availability, degradation, exposure and process progress as mutable state. Exact fields and equations remain open; 04’s vocabulary and values are candidates, not a 05 taxonomy. The transition overview further treats site, grape variety, vineyard development, weather and harvest as inputs to distinct wine potential, and rejects a single universal quality target. Current 05 source has no vineyard, weather, grape or harvest domain yet; README describes those systems as unimplemented.

## Sibling comparisons inspected

- **VisualIdle — land placement and production constraints.** `src/game/construction/BuildingPlacementRules.ts` takes a map snapshot and returns explicit placement failures for missing/occupied tiles, terrain, control and research ([8d18633](https://github.com/gram12321/visualidle/blob/8d186331e838ab49c82fb11d9452ba09a9e0b893/src/game/construction/BuildingPlacementRules.ts#L18)). `src/game/production/ProductionRules.ts` derives production work from terrain and distance, and checks stock, incoming transport and capacity ([ProductionRules.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/visualidle/blob/8d186331e838ab49c82fb11d9452ba09a9e0b893/src/game/production/ProductionRules.ts#L21)). Reuse the shape of pure, explainable site/work constraints and reason codes. These are building/map rules; neither source models soil suitability, vines, seasons, ripeness or harvest.
- **SkiClicker — weather changing a site condition.** `src/stores/weatherStore.ts` derives snow condition from current and previous weather; `src/engine/weather/WeatherSystem.ts` applies it to each slope and recalculates difficulty ([WeatherSystem.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/Skiclicker/blob/4cb5802d31ecb8858d0d4c2514d653ce7fd5cf0b/src/engine/weather/WeatherSystem.ts#L16)). The useful pattern is explicit previous-condition dependency before recalculating site outputs. Its seasonal sample uses `Math.random()` and ski/snow rules, so neither formulas nor random path meet 05’s reproducibility direction; there is no crop or agricultural weather model.
- **Office Tycoon — staff work attribution.** `src/engine/staff/staffEngine.ts` returns per-person contributions based on capacity, skill fit, specialization and assigned activity count, alongside a team total and XP basis ([staffEngine.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/officetycoon/blob/1cbabbc02ea1eb2950732b81e7f2dd2561cbd0be/src/engine/staff/staffEngine.ts#L104)). This can inform explainable work previews and awards, not vineyard work coefficients or weather constraints.
- **Industri Clicker — typed production inputs and quality.** `game/facilities/facilityProduction.ts` builds deterministic recipe input plans and amount-weights input quality; `facility.ts` owns constructed-facility and progress state (local checkout `9d3165a`). This illustrates separating input plans from production outcomes. It aggregates industrial resources; it has no grape variety, site suitability or vine growth.

No inspected sibling provides a direct analogue for perennial vine growth, variety-by-site fit, ripeness, yield, or a harvest window. Avoid inferring one from industrial facilities, terrain placement, or snow conditions.

## Minimal 05 direction to investigate

Keep vineyard rules in a vineyard domain. A minimal candidate is block area/density, vine age and health; site aspect/exposure, elevation and soil drainage/water retention; and variety-specific suitability, yield capacity and climate range. Advance a ripeness accumulator and stress state from dated weather using explicit reproducible inputs, then derive yield capacity and ripe availability. Keep Terroir Potential distinct from commercial Origin Prestige; ripeness is not a universal quality score. Harvest applies the minimum of ripe kg, feasible work and storage capacity, returning blockers and applied work so preview and execution share one calculation. Vineyard owns planted vines and crop availability; wine owns harvested lot composition/development; inventory owns quantity/location; the command/tick coordinator orders weather, vineyard update, work and committed harvest as agreed in `design.md` decisions 17–18 and 26–30. Exact attributes and coefficients need product design and calibration.

Meaningful future tests: identical date/site/seed and state produce identical updates, while forecast queries do not consume live progression; variety/site combinations change potential/readiness through named factors; unsuitable conditions reduce growth or health without changing Origin Prestige; harvest preview matches applied work under weather, staff and capacity limits; partial harvest never exceeds ripe yield and conserves grape quantity; and a lot harvested at week end first develops in the following interval. These are recommendations for eventual implementation, not tests run here.
