# Topic 12 — Commercial core loop

**05 direction:** Preserve sales, pricing, customers and contracts, reconnecting them to current wine assessment and Origin Prestige; keep Terroir Potential agricultural and commercial prestige distinct ([design.md](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md); [transition overview](../../docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_Transition_Overview.md), §§2.12, 6.7–7). Shared-market listings and peer settlement remain outside this company-local NPC slice ([transition-contracts.md](../transition-contracts.md), Scope).

**04 evidence (checkout `94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26`):** [wineScoreCalculation.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/wine/winescore/wineScoreCalculation.ts) averages taste and structure, then multiplies score, legacy land value, feature effects, company prestige and vineyard prestige. [generateOrder.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/sales/generateOrder.ts) stores a buyer-derived offer price and expiry, but mixes current valuation with mutable asking price, random rejection, and database/notification writes. [contractService.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/lib/services/sales/contractService.ts) rechecks wine requirements at delivery and pays the stored price. Its [AssignWineModal.tsx](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26/src/components/pages/sales/AssignWineModal.tsx) warns excess selection is unused, yet fulfillment consumes every submitted quantity and pays only the requested total; do not copy this split-write flow or over-allocation behavior.

**Current 05 seam:** [commercial/types.ts](../../src/game/commercial/types.ts) defines three NPC buyers, relationship and purchase totals, fixed-cent contracts with grape/structure/taste/age requirements and due/status fields, plus sales. [Commands.ts](../../src/game/core/Commands.ts) accepts no client price. [CommercialActions.ts](../../src/game/commands/CommercialActions.ts) derives sale quotes, protects reserved stock, requires exact full contract reservation, rechecks each lot at delivery, then returns inventory, payment, sale and status effects in one candidate. [CompanyView.ts](../../src/game/queries/CompanyView.ts) exposes buyer quotes and eligible lots. [wine/types.ts](../../src/game/wine/types.ts) stores each blend source’s origin and fraction; source-weighted Origin Prestige is available. Required pure APIs are `createContractOffers`, `advanceContracts`, `quoteWine`, `contractEligibility` and `recordSale` in [transition-contracts.md](../transition-contracts.md). `src/game/commercial/Rules.ts` is absent in this checkout, though actions, queries and the weekly advance already import it; rule behavior and relationship outcomes remain unverified.

**Proposed take and critical tests:** Quote contextual quality, buyer taste/style fit and maturity; apply the fraction-weighted Origin Prestige of all blend sources exactly once. Freeze the authority-generated offer price, revalidate eligibility at reservation and delivery, and resolve due/default plus relationship consequences once per week/outcome. Add focused quote, source-weighting and offer-repeatability cases in `tests/game/commercial.test.ts`; extend `tests/game/core-loop.test.ts` and `tests/game/inventory.test.ts` for multi-lot reservation, over-allocation, eligibility drift and unchanged state on failed settlement; extend `tests/api/game-loop.test.ts` and `tests/game/time-advance.test.ts` for receipt-safe fulfillment and one-time due/default processing.
