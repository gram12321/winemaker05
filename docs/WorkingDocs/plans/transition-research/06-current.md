# Topic 06 — Taste families and descriptors

In 05, [evaluation/types.ts](../../src/game/evaluation/types.ts#L3) selects eight families (`redFruit`, `darkFruit`, `citrus`, `floral`, `herbal`, `earth`, `oak`, `aged`) and keeps profile, fit, quality, faults, and descriptors as separate concepts. [wine/types.ts](../../src/game/wine/types.ts#L25) provides current physical state and fraction-weighted immutable grape sources. No `Taste.ts` implementation exists yet; [transition-contracts.md](../transition-contracts.md#L22) assigns Topic06 a pure family and descriptor projection from current material and grape identity. [Sensory.ts](../../src/game/evaluation/Sensory.ts#L29) is the existing precedent for a derived current projection separate from stored state and evaluation.

Approved 05 direction keeps a parallel taste moving-target system, distinguishes structural balance, taste fit, style, typicity, and faults, and leaves the family/descriptor taxonomy and overall-score contribution open ([design.md](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md#L12); [transition overview](../../docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_Transition_Overview.md#L978)). The 04 counts of 14 families and 26 descriptors are references, not requirements ([design.md](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md#L121)).

04’s [wineTasteProfileService.ts](C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/services/wine/taste/wineTasteProfileService.ts#L49) derives families from structure channels, anchors, red/white grape classification, and active feature severity, then derives descriptor values and a family map. Direct callers include [tasteQualityIndexService.ts](C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/services/wine/taste/tasteQualityIndexService.ts#L54), which scores families against color/grape targets and inter-family rules; [WineTasteProfilePanel.tsx](C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/components/ui/components/WineTasteProfilePanel.tsx#L107), where descriptors are display-only; and [wineAnchorImpactDebugService.ts](C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/services/wine/debug/wineAnchorImpactDebugService.ts#L164), which exposes anchor impact breakdowns. The migration row recommends reviewing this breakdown while independently designing 05’s projection and reusing the shared evaluator ([System Migration Research](../../docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_System_Migration_Research.md#L141)).

**Proposed take:** Make 05’s profile a deterministic pure projection of `PhysicalState` plus fraction-weighted grape IDs in `sources`; derive descriptors from that same material/profile. Do not feed targets, fit scores, buyer preferences, or intended quality into identity labels. Treat descriptors as character, not automatic quality; assess fit and faults separately.

Key scenarios: identical lot inputs always yield the same profile regardless of buyer/targets; changing oak, maturation, or age can shift oak/aged descriptors without mutating the lot; a blend preserves each source grape identity according to its fraction; a prominent family can sit outside the contextual target and score low fit, while physical faults remain separately assessed.
