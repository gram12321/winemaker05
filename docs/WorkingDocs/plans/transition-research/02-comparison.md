# 02 - Wine anchors: sibling comparison beyond Winemaker04

## Evidence

The approved 15 November design keeps inherited raw-material potential as a harvest baseline, with remaining availability, degradation, exposure and process progress in mutable development state. It assigns wine composition and development to the lot, quantity and location to inventory, and vessel properties to equipment; exact fields and equations remain open (`docs/WorkingDocs/design.md`, decisions 26-30). The migration research's R72 row describes Industri Clicker as a scalar, quantity-weighted input quality plus quality ceilings, explicitly distinguishing it from wine-specific inherited and mutable anchors (`docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_System_Migration_Research.md:137,476`).

## Industri Clicker comparison

I inspected local Industri Clicker `main` at `9d3165a786403e3072f65d54fdb0a74e91ec673b`; the migration row's pinned `a6c85c0` revision is an ancestor of this checkout. `C:/GitHub/industriClicker/game/quality/qualityMath.ts:76-106` computes one scalar weighted input Q and limits output Q by the minimum of input, research, facility upgrade, company production, staff and numeric ceilings. Its `OutputQualityBreakdown` preserves those sources, making the active bottleneck explainable. This is a strong pattern for traceable derivation, but the axes are industrial production gates, not separate inherited material potentials. They collapse to one output score, and the input bonus, output bonus and multiplier are recipe/quality rules, not physical development.

`C:/GitHub/industriClicker/game/facilities/facilityProduction.ts:107-120,252-270` builds amount-based input plans, calculates weighted input quality and source cost, then consumes planned stock when a cycle starts. This separates planned material amounts from production work and output quality. However, `C:/GitHub/industriClicker/game/inventory/inventory.ts:6-23,63-105` keeps one aggregate entry per resource type: quantity, one quality and source cost. Adding stock averages quality by amount; removing stock reduces quantity only. There are no distinct lots, inherited origin records, dimensions with remaining extractable reserves, or lot-specific histories in this path. The sibling therefore does not supply the wine analogue itself.

## Recommendation for 05

Use three explicit concepts. First, keep a traceable set of wine-specific Raw Material Anchors as the immutable harvest baseline, with provenance back to the harvest inputs. Think of these as inherited potentials or ceilings across selected dimensions, not a commitment to 04's 12 fields or a second inventory. Second, put mutable physical development in the lot: current measured state, process progress, exposure/degradation and only the finite reservoirs that the wine model actually needs. Where a process draws down material, retain a clear relation between inherited potential and remaining amount; do not make every anchor a consumable reservoir. Third, keep physical inventory quantity, unit, location and custody separate from concentrations, capacities and quality. Lot splitting and blending then need explicit lineage and material balance while allowing separate descendants to develop differently, consistent with decisions 26-30.

Adopt Industri's explainable input/output breakdown and deterministic amount plan as comparison patterns, not its scalar quality model or resource-wide aggregation. The missing wine analogue is multidimensional, harvest-inherited potential that can be selectively transformed through extraction, fermentation and maturation while lot quantity remains separately conserved. Designing that causal link is 05 work; the approved design leaves its fields, units and equations open.
