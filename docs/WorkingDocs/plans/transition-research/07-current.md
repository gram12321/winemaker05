# Topic 07 — Assessment and quality

Approved 05 direction keeps contextual moving targets for structure and taste, distinguishes balance, taste fit, style, typicity, and faults, and leaves their overall contributions open ([design.md](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md#L12), decisions 31 and 39). The active [contract](../transition-contracts.md#L23) assigns `assessWine(lot)`; current [evaluation types](../../src/game/evaluation/types.ts) already define separate structure/taste assessments, quality, faults, and descriptors. [MovingTargets.ts](../../src/game/evaluation/MovingTargets.ts) implements a shared bounded `evaluateProfile`; [Sensory.ts](../../src/game/evaluation/Sensory.ts) derives structure from current state. At inspection, `Taste.ts`, `Profiles.ts`, and `Assessment.ts` were not present, although command/query callers already expect `assessWine` ([WineActions.ts](../../src/game/commands/WineActions.ts), [CommercialActions.ts](../../src/game/commands/CommercialActions.ts), [CompanyView.ts](../../src/game/queries/CompanyView.ts)). [PhysicalLot](../../src/game/wine/types.ts) retains current physical state and a separate numeric bottling snapshot; [WineActions.ts](../../src/game/commands/WineActions.ts) captures current scores at bottling, while other callers recalculate current assessments. The snapshot stores week plus structure, taste, and quality only; historical fault details are not retained.

At Winemaker04 HEAD `94fd950f0e63287f748ac5c0d7a66309add36e26`, [tasteQualityIndexService.ts](C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/services/wine/taste/tasteQualityIndexService.ts) applies red/white family targets, grape nudges, profile-dependent target rules, and a weighted 14-family score (including a `faults` family). [wineScoreCalculation.ts](C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/services/wine/winescore/wineScoreCalculation.ts) averages that taste index with stored `structureIndex`; estimated price then applies land, feature, and prestige multipliers. [gameTick.ts](C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/services/core/gameTick.ts) updates live indices, while [wineLogService.ts](C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/features/wineLog/services/wineLogService.ts) prefers bottling snapshots for history. Migration research therefore calls for redesign and explicitly rejects copying the 50/50 average ([System Migration Research](../../docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_System_Migration_Research.md#L143)).

**Proposed take:** Keep `deriveTaste` a pure projection of current physical state and fraction-weighted grape sources, with descriptors descriptive rather than scored. Build both target profiles through bounded, explained rules; use `diagnoseWine` for separate physical faults, not another structural or taste penalty. A provisional summary can use `quality = sqrt(structure.score * taste.score)`, so a strong fit cannot fully mask a weak one; keep fault, style, typicity, and commercial contributions explicit and open to tuning.

Critical scenarios: identical lots produce deterministic profiles/descriptors; changing targets changes fit but not the sensory projection; midpoint, edge, out-of-range, and extreme target cases remain bounded and explained; skewed structure/taste fits remain visible alongside quality; fault diagnoses are deterministic and separate; bottling preserves its week/scores while subsequent development changes current assessment without rewriting the snapshot.
