# Winemaker04 to Winemaker05 System Inventory

**Status:** Working migration assessment; recommendations are not all approved 05 design.
**Reviewed:** 2026-11-13

## Purpose and decision boundary

The [transition overview](Winemaker04_to_05_Transition_Overview.md) owns the redesigned vineyard-to-wine loop. This inventory covers the supporting systems and their connections to that loop: company setup, time, work, staff, finance, progression, markets, persistence, records, and player utilities.

The [approved 05 design](design.md) establishes the cellar-centered, causal wine-development direction and 2.5D scene and panel direction, and separates Terroir Potential from Origin Prestige. It does not choose the UI technology or save provider. This inventory keeps those facts separate from the system recommendations below.

The user directions recorded in this review are:

- Keep the in-game calendar and game tick. Winemaker05 will not use a server tick. Revisit the orchestration and code boundaries; this is not a 1:1 code migration.
- Carry the authored 04 starting scenarios, starting team members, and story content over as closely as possible, then map them to 05 fields.
- Keep and refine land and vineyard assets.
- Keep weather ideas and its crop/harvest links, while redesigning its presentation and reviewing its effects.
- Let the transition overview own grape/harvest, wine-development, and cellar/maturation mechanics.
- Keep most activity behavior and the richer Winemaker staff model as the primary baseline. Keep staff training within staff progression and work experience.
- Treat finance, lenders, credit rating, and loans as one package. Use Winemaker04 as the fullest winery-specific baseline and compare trimmed implementations.
- Defer a separate research-system decision. Grants, gates, and lasting effects may belong to other 05 systems.
- Keep Winemaker04 prestige as the baseline and compare sibling-game adaptations.
- Keep achievements, production history, and notifications, with the history model tied to the 05 maturity design.
- Keep the ability to save company progress. Supabase is a reference and possible provider; the 05 backend, schema, and sync model depend on the eventual stack and play mode.
- Keep useful NPC, local, and company-to-company buying and selling for grapes and storage vessels, then consider broader market coverage as 05 inventory and trading rules are defined.

## How to read the recommendations

- **Carry as content:** transfer authored scenario, character, or story data with only field mapping and balance changes required by 05.
- **Keep behavior; refactor boundaries:** retain the player choice and useful outcome, then place rules, orchestration, presentation, and persistence according to the 05 architecture.
- **Rebuild in 05:** keep the player-facing purpose, but replace mechanics, state, formulas, or presentation where they conflict with the approved 05 direction.
- **Follow the transition overview:** preserve the gameplay responsibility, but design its rules under the agreed 05 wine loop.
- **Adapt a sibling pattern:** use the named implementation as evidence for one useful behavior or data pattern; it is not a commitment to use that game's stack or formulas.
- **Defer:** keep the option open until a product or platform decision resolves it.
- **Omit:** leave it out unless 05 acquires a clear need.

A 1:1 transfer can mean authored content or player behavior. Source code, formulas, data enums, UI components, and database tables are separate choices.

## System and subsystem inventory

### New company, calendar, and world

- **Calendar and game tick — Keep; refactor the orchestration.** Retain the player-visible winery calendar and explicit game-tick advancement. Keep simulation time inside the game; do not use a server tick. The 04 tick coordinates weather, crop changes, activities, wages, finance, wine development, contracts, markets, and progression. In 05, make the tick coordinate domain updates while each domain owns its calculations and state changes. Keep the 05 update order and any changed boundaries open until the wine loop is designed. See the [04 game tick](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/services/core/gameTick.ts).

- **Company identity, profile, and preferences — Defer the account model; retain the capability.** 04 has users, authentication, company records, profiles, and company preferences. Retain the ability to create and identify a company and save player preferences. Decide account identity, company ownership, and save slots with the 05 persistence and play-mode decisions. See the [04 user feature](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/user) and [company feature](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/company).

- **Starting scenarios, characters, and story — Carry as content.** Preserve the named starting scenarios, family and mentor writing, starting staff and teams, land and vineyard setup, money and loans, prestige, and initial grape or research access as authored 04 content. Map each field to 05; rebalance only where the new wine loop or a removed system requires it. Story and starting conditions are authored game content, separate from a guided tutorial. See [04 starting-condition data](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/constants/startingConditions.ts), [starting-condition setup](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/company/services), and [04 story documents](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/docs/Story). If separate research is deferred, review the scenario's starting research IDs instead of importing them as working 05 unlocks.

- **Guided tutorial and panel onboarding — Add the system; adapt sibling behavior to 05.** Keep story content separate from tutorial flow. [IndustriClicker's tutorial state](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/39e1d68db71107ef3b1263f853be2d618cf9ec7f/game/tutorial/tutorialState.ts) and [tutorial dialog](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/39e1d68db71107ef3b1263f853be2d618cf9ec7f/ui/dashboard/components/dialog/TutorialDialog.tsx) provide typed steps across Company, Production, Facility, and Inventory; dimmed spotlights; a collapsible and dismissible guide; back, next, exit, and jump controls; and checks when a facility changes during a lesson. Its persisted tutorial state records welcome completion, while the active step is transient. [Simulus01's tutorial service](https://github.com/gram12321/Simulus01/blob/c39631701b0a1dcbb8ae0d13993747f5ca929336/src/lib/tutorial/tutorialService.ts) and [element highlight](https://github.com/gram12321/Simulus01/blob/c39631701b0a1dcbb8ae0d13993747f5ca929336/src/components/tutorial/ElementHighlight.tsx) add a lesson registry, previous/next actions, per-company seen state, and element highlighting. These are useful behavior references, not a general panel-visibility manager: 05 needs shell-level tutorial actions to navigate to a destination, open or reveal the target panel, highlight a scene or panel target, temporarily hide unrelated UI when useful, and restore the prior view on exit. This fits the approved Estate, Winery, Cellar, Company, and Management destinations and the hideable Activity Manager; see the [alpha UI plan](plans/2026-11-13-alpha-ui-design-plan.md). The siblings use React Native or DOM targets, so their components and selectors are not 05 implementation choices while the stack and 2.5D renderer remain undecided.

- **Land and vineyard assets — Keep and refine.** Retain land search and acquisition, site and region data, parcel capacity, clearing and overgrowth, planting and density, vine age, health, condition, yield potential, and ownership. Revisit site and value rules against the approved Terroir Potential and Origin Prestige distinction. Do not reuse the 04 Land Value Modifier as a single substitute for both concepts. See the [04 vineyard services](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/services/vineyard).

- **Weather and forecasts — Keep the ideas; redesign presentation and review effects.** Retain forecasts, seasonal outlooks, and weather consequences for ripeness, vine health, planting and harvest work, and any useful market effects. The 04 weather code separates resolution, vineyard effects, operation effects, market effects, and presentation, but the current user-facing presentation is unsatisfactory. Rework the visual and explanatory treatment for 05, including how a forecast communicates uncertainty and which vineyard or harvest decisions it changes. Keep its effects connected to the crop and harvest rules in the [transition overview](Winemaker04_to_05_Transition_Overview.md). See the [04 weather feature](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/weather).

### Vineyard-to-wine loop and work

- **Grape growth, yield, and harvest — Follow the transition overview.** The subsystem includes vine growth and condition, ripeness, yield, harvest timing, and partial harvesting. Preserve those player decisions as part of the 05 loop; redesign the calculations and state flow with the new wine model. See the [04 vineyard manager](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/services/vineyard/vineyardManager.ts) and [weather-to-vineyard service](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/weather/weatherVineyardService.ts).

- **Wine production, sensory development, and faults — Follow the transition overview.** Keep the production capability and the meaningful process choices. Rebuild the material, process, sensory, evaluation, fault, and lifecycle models under the agreed causal development direction. The 05 inventory must not inherit 04 formulas or persisted lifecycle states just because they exist. See the [transition overview](Winemaker04_to_05_Transition_Overview.md).

- **Cellar, vessels, and maturation — Split asset logistics from wine development.** Keep cellar capacity, vessel ownership, vessel types, condition, cleanliness, fill history, occupancy, allocation, reservation, and maintenance as useful winery management. Design maturation and bottle development with the new 05 wine engine, where storage conditions cause wine changes. Use the transition overview for wine state and maturity behavior; use 04 equipment services as the logistics reference. See the [04 vessel types](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/types/storageVessels.ts), [vessel allocation](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/services/wine/winery/storageVesselAllocationService.ts), and [vessel maintenance](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/services/wine/winery/storageVesselMaintenanceService.ts).

- **Activities and work completion — Keep most player behavior close to 04; refactor task ownership.** Preserve the work queue's useful choices: selecting a task, assigning staff, estimating work, tracking partial progress, viewing previews, pausing, resuming, cancelling, and applying the completion result. Keep task types 05 still needs, such as vineyard care, clearing and planting, harvest, crushing, fermentation setup, vessel upkeep, bookkeeping, and staff, land, or lender searches. Research, purchasing, and task types tied to removed progression depend on their 05 dispositions. Route each completed action to the domain that owns its result; let staff progression own the experience earned by that work. The sibling audit found no newer general queue that combines finite staff-assigned tasks, task-specific work calculations, and staff experience on completion. IndustriClicker models continuous facility production and individual timed work such as lender search; Simulus01 uses aggregated worker capacity. These are comparison points for workload and presentation, not replacements for the 04 activity model. See the [04 activity feature](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/activities), [work calculators](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/activities/services/workcalculators), and [activity manager](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/activities/services/activitymanagers/activityManager.ts).

- **Staff, teams, wages, and experience — Keep Winemaker04 as the main baseline; compare simpler labor models.** The 04 staff capability includes hiring and candidate search, employee profiles, skills and specialist roles, teams and default assignments, work assignment, availability, wages, and experience by skill, task, and grape familiarity. Keep development connected to completed work. Put training inside staff progression and work experience rather than creating a separate 05 system. IndustriClicker is a useful trimmed comparison: it assigns worker counts to facilities and tracks shared staff quality, training, target wages, and staffing changes, but has no named employee skill or team model comparable to Winemaker04. Skiclicker and Simulus01 also model aggregate labor, not wine-industry specialists. Decide whether 05 needs every 04 skill, team, and familiarity track after reviewing how each changes work choices. See the [04 staff services](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/staff/services), [teams](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/staff/services/teamDefinitions.ts), and [work experience calculations](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/activities/services/workcalculators/workCalculator.ts).

### Finance and progression

- **Finance, company value, lenders, credit rating, and loans — Keep as one business finance package.** Include the transaction ledger, cash and asset values, income and expenses, company value, financial statements, wages, lender search and offers, credit rating, borrowing limits, payment schedules, missed payments, emergency loans, restructuring, default, and any asset-loss path that 05 wants. Preserve 04 as the fullest winery-specific baseline. Compare its mechanics and presentation with IndustriClicker's typed transaction and credit breakdowns, and Skiclicker's trimmed loan and credit implementation. These implementations provide narrower state and report patterns; they do not justify replacing the winery package wholesale. Keep work cost and staff assignment in the activity/staff domains, with loan state and financial effects owned by the finance package. Recalculate 05 assets and default consequences from 05 needs. See the [04 finance services](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/services/finance), [finance database modules](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/database/core), and [04 lender and loan feature](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/loanLender).

- **Research projects and a separate research screen — Defer.** The 04 research feature combines paid, staff-worked projects with grape and technology unlocks, capability effects, grants, eligibility checks, and permanent effects. The causal 05 wine loop may make some of these projects redundant. Do not carry the 04 research feature or its UI as an assumed system. Decide whether 05 needs a distinct research activity after the process, cellar, and capability progression are mapped. IndustriClicker's typed project/effect ledger is useful if 05 keeps research. VisualIdle's tree is a visual reference only; its age-column progression and technology nodes serve a different game. The alpha UI plan leaves Research / Growth out of required navigation and does not approve research mechanics.

- **Grants and funding — Decide separately from research.** 04 projects can award cash, prestige, unlocks, and permanent effects. Define each surviving grant as funding, a one-use action reward, or a progression effect, and let the relevant 05 system own it. IndustriClicker demonstrates both cash rewards in its research catalog and a separate durable one-use grant ledger that waives a specified action cost. These are different mechanics; only adopt one if it supports a winery decision.

- **Eligibility gates and blocking — Reassign each gate to its actual owner.** 04 research eligibility can depend on company age or value, prestige, buyer loyalty, achievements, or prerequisite projects. Preserve only conditions that create clear 05 progression. If a surviving gate belongs to a technique, cellar capability, market, or company progression system, keep it there instead of rebuilding a general research blocker.

- **Permanent effects and upgrades — Assign effects to the domain they change.** 04 stores permanent research effects in a common service. In 05, a durable change to wine processes, vineyard capability, staff, or markets should be defined and applied by the domain that owns that behavior. A shared effect catalog can be considered after the 05 capabilities are known.

- **Prestige and reputation — Keep the 04 prestige system as the baseline; adapt selectively.** Preserve the source and explanation of earned prestige, its persistence or decay where that still fits, and its effects on company progression and commerce. The 05 term Origin Prestige is a separate agreed origin premium. Do not merge it with broad company prestige, winery or brand reputation, buyer loyalty, or creditworthiness without an explicit design decision. Skiclicker states that its prestige model was adapted from older Winemaker; it adds persisted event upserts, daily decay, and a reputation value used by demand. IndustriClicker's event ledger offers idempotent sourced events and derived decay. Compare these additions and keep the best fit; the 04 event catalog and formulas still need to be evaluated against 05. See the [04 prestige services](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/prestige/services), [Skiclicker prestige service](https://github.com/gram12321/Skiclicker/blob/4cb5802d31ecb8858d0d4c2514d653ce7fd5cf0b/src/engine/prestige/prestigeService.ts), [Skiclicker prestige store](https://github.com/gram12321/Skiclicker/blob/4cb5802d31ecb8858d0d4c2514d653ce7fd5cf0b/src/stores/prestigeStore.ts), and [IndustriClicker prestige ledger](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/prestige/prestige.ts).

- **Achievements and milestones — Keep; rebuild their checks around 05 events.** Keep the 04 achievement goals, tiers, progress feedback, unlock records, and rewards that suit 05. Evaluate them from real 05 progress, and let them remain readable goals rather than hidden gates by default. IndustriClicker has a code-backed metric evaluator and saved unlock ledger that can inform evaluation and persistence. Skiclicker's current achievement service calls itself a placeholder and tracks unlocks in memory. The reviewed ProtoProduction main and distinct codespace branch tips contain no runtime achievement system, so they are not verified donors. See the [04 achievement definitions](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/achievements/achievementDefinitions.ts), [IndustriClicker achievement evaluator](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/achievements/achievementEvaluator.ts), and [achievement ledger](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/achievements/achievement.ts).

- **Wine history and production records — Keep; redesign around 05 lots and maturity.** Preserve traceability and useful historical snapshots of a vintage and its production. 04 wine history records bottling and production details, scores, and vineyard summaries. In 05, record the path through raw material, lot transformations, vessel and storage, maturity, bottling, and sale from the model defined in the transition overview. Do not freeze maturity fields until that model is settled. See the [04 wine log service](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/features/wineLog).

- **Notifications — Keep the utility; adapt its event history and controls.** Retain durable game-date messages with category and source information. 04 supports category and origin filtering, but its notification record has no read or dismissed state. Simulus01's console persists timestamped, typed, categorized messages per company and has toast and notification preferences. TraderGame02 adds a Supabase history and saved filters for category or origin; read and dismissed flags are held by its service/UI, not by its notification database rows. TraderGame04's current console is in-memory, so it is not a persistence donor. A 05 notification layer should accept events from game domains, retain the useful event history and filtering, and let presentation preferences control toasts separately. See the [04 notification service](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/services/core/notificationService.ts), [Simulus01 console](https://github.com/gram12321/Simulus01/blob/c39631701b0a1dcbb8ae0d13993747f5ca929336/src/components/layout/Console.tsx), [Simulus01 settings](https://github.com/gram12321/Simulus01/blob/c39631701b0a1dcbb8ae0d13993747f5ca929336/src/components/views/SettingsView.tsx), and [TraderGame02 notification service](https://github.com/gram12321/tradergame02/blob/9de4106e3ca8ccdaef893571ab01745acf210068/src/lib/services/core/notificationService.ts).

### Market system inventory

The 04 shared buy-market interface currently registers two goods domains: grapes and storage vessels. The wider market and sales code covers local suppliers, NPC offers, global NPC grape listings, grape buyers, wine customers, company listings, and contract sales. The 05 direction in this review expects more complete market coverage; keep the shared capability extensible and add goods domains as the cellar and commercial loop define them.

| Market subsystem | Verified 04 coverage | 05 disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Shared buying interface and offer lifecycle | The 04 buy-market registry and offer lifecycle support product-specific domains, rather than one data model for all goods. | Keep common offer, expiry, purchase, and counterparty behavior where it fits; leave prices and inventory rules with each goods domain. |
| Local NPC suppliers | Local offers, seasonal refresh, supplier relationships, and purchases cover grapes and storage vessels. | Keep local sourcing and supplier relationship choices; decide how locality affects price, quality, availability, and weather exposure in 05. |
| Global NPC supply | Global grape listings and batch purchases provide a second sourcing route; storage vessels have separate supplier paths. | Keep sourcing/import choices where they matter and add other goods only after 05 inventories and ownership are defined. |
| Grape sales to NPC buyers | Grape buyer offers, purchase paths, and buyer loyalty are separate from the shared buy-market interface. | Keep as a sales domain if grape lots remain tradeable; reconnect offers and contracts to 05 lot identity, quality, timing, and storage. |
| Wine customers and direct sales | Customer generation and relationships, order offers, price calculation, sale fulfillment, and wine inventory are spread across sales services. | Keep the useful customer and portfolio decisions, then adapt price and fit to the 05 wine-development and cellar model. |
| Forward and customer contracts | 04 has wine contracts, forward grape/must contracts, contract generation, expiration/default handling, and cooperatives. | Preserve useful commitment and fulfillment decisions; rework requirements around 05 lot state and ensure capacity or inventory is reserved consistently. |
| Company-to-company listings | Product-specific company listings exist for grapes and storage vessels. They do not form a complete exchange for every winery asset, and settlement behavior must be checked per product path. | Keep listing concepts and expand product coverage if 05 includes peer trading. Define ownership transfer, inventory reservation, partial fills, cancellation, expiry, settlement, and transaction history for each goods domain. |
| Storage and cellar equipment | The 04 shared buy interface covers storage vessels, including supplier offers and used-vessel listings; it does not establish a market for every storage facility. | Keep vessel sourcing. Add cellar equipment or capacity markets after the 05 cellar inventory is defined. |
| Price formation and market movement | 04 uses product-specific buyer/supplier pricing, relationships, and some weather effects; it has no shared local-to-global price-diffusion model for all goods. | Compare IndustriClicker and TraderGame04 supply, quality, and price-diffusion patterns if changing prices make useful winery choices. Keep simulated prices separate from peer listing prices. |
| Later market domains | Land, staff search, and equipment suppliers are specific services, not one universal exchange. | Consider wine lots, bottled wine, additional cellar assets, equipment, and other goods only when their 05 ownership and trading rules are clear. |

Use [TraderGame01's transactional listing purchases](https://github.com/gram12321/tradergame01/blob/45ada828bd88eea11fba2cb2d62fcfe95049ef5e/src/lib/firebase/marketService.ts) as a settlement checklist; its purchase flow uses a database transaction, validates the listing and quantities, moves funds and inventory, and supports partial quantity purchases. Use [CivIdle's trade logic](https://github.com/gram12321/CivIdle/blob/de9fec6494e102d7935de92b84b3ca6a19a264e6/shared/logic/PlayerTradeLogic.ts) for route, quantity, account-limit, and trade-term ideas. CivIdle is GPL-3.0, so use it as design evidence unless 05's license decision supports code reuse. [TraderGame02's marketplace](https://github.com/gram12321/tradergame02/blob/9de4106e3ca8ccdaef893571ab01745acf210068/docs/market_system_implementation.md) supports listing and browsing with live updates; purchase remains a stub. It is not a working settlement reference. IndustriClicker and TraderGame04 model simulated supply and price behavior; they do not provide a general player-to-player exchange.

### Persistence and supporting systems

- **Game saves and Supabase — Keep persistent saves; defer the provider and schema.** 04 uses Supabase for authentication and company-scoped game records across activities, finance, staff, weather, markets, research, notifications, and wine history. Retain the ability to save a company and its progress. Decide local or cloud storage, shared-market authority, authentication, sync, and transaction boundaries with the 05 play model. Keep Supabase as an option; do not treat 04 tables or data shapes as 05 requirements. A shared marketplace may need remote state and atomic settlement even when simulation time is not advanced by a server. Compare [IndustriClicker's per-company SQLite snapshots](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/core/persistence/gameSaveDatabase.ts) and [VisualIdle's versioned local save](https://github.com/gram12321/visualidle/blob/692e6ba6963ce58029dfa908a7c5a76b9da484cd/src/app/GamePersistence.ts). TraderGame04's server tick is outside the 05 direction; its persistence and market code do not change the no-server-tick decision. See the [04 Supabase client](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/database/core/supabase.ts) and [04 database modules](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/tree/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/database).

- **High scores and online leaderboards — Defer.** 04 has cloud rankings for company, wine, and vineyard. Decide whether public competition and shared player identity are part of 05. ## Suggested 05 responsibility seams

These are proposals to guide later architecture work; they do not select a framework or database.

- Let the calendar and game tick provide simulation time and coordinate updates. Keep weather, crop, work, wine, finance, and market rules in their owning domains.
- Let guided onboarding navigate to a 05 scene or panel, reveal and highlight its target, and restore the prior view when a step exits. Keep tutorial state separate from scenario and story content.
- Let Activities own assignment, time spent, progress, and task lifecycle. Let the completion operation call the domain that owns the result.
- Keep staff profiles, teams, skill growth, and work experience together. Let finance record wages and the resulting company transactions.
- Let research, grants, gates, achievements, and prestige be separate capabilities unless 05 design deliberately combines them.
- Give market goods domains their own inventory and pricing rules while sharing listing and settlement contracts where they truly match.
- Persist game state through a save adapter chosen for 05. Emit player notifications from domain events and keep toast preferences in presentation settings.
- Let wine history record traceable lot events and state snapshots from the new maturity model.

## Decisions still open

1. The 05 save model and provider: Supabase, local saves, synced saves, or a combination; authentication, company ownership, and save slots.
2. The exact 05 tick period and ordering of domain updates. The in-game calendar and game tick remain; a server tick is outside the direction.
3. Whether a distinct research tree is useful after the new wine loop and capability progression are specified.
4. Where surviving grants, blocking conditions, and lasting effects belong.
5. Whether 05 keeps the full 04 staff skill, team, and familiarity breadth or reduces some tracks. Keep training inside staff progression either way.
6. How broad public and peer markets should be, which product categories come first, and how listing reservations, partial fills, settlement, cancellation, expiry, and history work.
7. How Winemaker prestige interacts with Origin Prestige, brand or winery reputation, buyer loyalty, and credit.
8. Which history fields and maturity snapshots follow the new lot model.
9. How tutorial stages target 05 scenes and management panels, control temporary visibility, restore navigation state, and persist completion or progress.
10. Whether public leaderboards or company ownership features have a 05 purpose.
