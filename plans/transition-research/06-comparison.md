# 06 - Taste families and descriptors

The approved direction is to derive current sensory profiles from current wine state and assess taste with the shared moving-target framework; taxonomy, projection, descriptor rules, and score weight remain open ([design.md](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md#L99), [taste inventory row](../../docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_System_Migration_Research.md#L141)). Current 05 defines eight provisional `TasteFamily` keys and a numeric `TasteProfile`, while `WineAssessment` only types descriptors as strings ([types.ts](../../src/game/evaluation/types.ts#L1)). `Sensory.ts` projects physical state into six structural axes; it contains no taste-family or descriptor derivation ([Sensory.ts](../../src/game/evaluation/Sensory.ts#L6)). `evaluateProfile` accepts generic numeric values and targets, so it can score a future taste profile without defining its sensory identity ([MovingTargets.ts](../../src/game/evaluation/MovingTargets.ts#L50)).

The pinned Industri implementation computes quantity-weighted input quality, applies the tightest quality ceiling, and returns a scalar output breakdown ([qualityMath.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/quality/qualityMath.ts#L76-L106)). Its traceable inputs are a useful reporting pattern, but it has no family projection or descriptors. WhiskeyMixer's pinned batch initializer sets `flavor: []` with a TODO to assign it from source/quality ([gameStore.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/WhiskeyMixer/blob/8cebe03fbe2ed19715a3825d50c19119c14d58f6/src/store/gameStore.ts#L97-L114)); its age-based quality update does not fill that gap. Neither sibling establishes a wine-taste analogue.

Keep sensory identity and descriptors as a deterministic projection of current lot state, independent of targets and score; evaluate that projection separately. Do not freeze the current eight keys or borrow the 04 taxonomy while those choices remain open. Extend the existing style in [sensory.test.ts](../../tests/game/sensory.test.ts): assert repeatability, bounds, no mutation, blend-order invariance, and that changing target ranges or weights changes fit without changing profile/descriptors. Once descriptor thresholds are approved, test their exact boundaries and stable tie ordering.
