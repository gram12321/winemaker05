# Topic 10 comparison

The 05 implementation contract controls: `developLot` advances integer days in the canonical order fermentation/extraction, maturation, risk, then age; wine owns sugar, alcohol, yeast and finite phenolics, while inventory owns amounts ([transition-contracts.md](../transition-contracts.md), [types.ts](../../src/game/wine/types.ts), [Operations.ts](../../src/game/wine/Operations.ts)). These comparisons inform accounting and step mechanics, not scientific constants.

Industri Clicker's [recipeTypes.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/recipes/recipeTypes.ts#L52) defines required and optional/grouped inputs, outputs and deterministic work. Its [facilityProduction.ts input planning](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/facilities/facilityProduction.ts#L73) builds an input plan; the [cycle handler](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/facilities/facilityProduction.ts#L252) blocks a cycle when required stock is short, consumes selected inputs at cycle start, tracks work, then adds outputs at completion. Reuse this explicit-plan/accounting pattern to expose feasibility and material deltas. Do not copy its discrete commodity cycle or [quality ceilings](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/quality/qualityMath.ts#L92) as fermentation: neither represents partial sugar conversion or depletion of an extractable reservoir.

WhiskeyMixer is only an age projection: its [gameStore.ts](https://github.com/gram12321/WhiskeyMixer/blob/8cebe03fbe2ed19715a3825d50c19119c14d58f6/src/store/gameStore.ts#L71) recalculates batch quality from base quality and age each week, and reduces cask condition; it has no conversion or extraction state. Its [time.ts age calculation](https://github.com/gram12321/WhiskeyMixer/blob/8cebe03fbe2ed19715a3825d50c19119c14d58f6/src/utils/time.ts#L40) counts 52 weeks per year although the same [calendar](https://github.com/gram12321/WhiskeyMixer/blob/8cebe03fbe2ed19715a3825d50c19119c14d58f6/src/utils/time.ts#L3) has 12 weeks across four seasons. Treat it as an age-only example and avoid its year-boundary calculation.

For Topic 10, preserve daily material accounting: do not consume sugar below the 4 g/L threshold; derive alcohol only from sugar actually consumed using a named provisional yield; reduce remaining phenolics as extraction occurs without exceeding inherited capacity. Critical scenarios are exact sugar/alcohol deltas and caps; low-yeast or adverse-temperature stalls that retain sugar until restart; no extraction without skin contact; repeated extraction within finite capacity; seven daily calls matching one seven-day call under constant conditions; and a mid-run environment change producing a different, still-valid result. Also assert maturation and risk preserve fermentation-owned fields and that the engine returns one consolidated lot. These tests establish deterministic game rules, not enological accuracy.
