# 03 comparison: Terroir Potential and Origin Prestige

The approved 05 boundary is agricultural Terroir Potential versus commercial Origin Prestige; their formulas and relationship to vineyard/company prestige are open ([design](../../docs/WorkingDocs/design.md), decisions 10 and 40). The transition overview records why this split matters: 04's Land Value Modifier combines normalized land value and vineyard prestige, then the same modifier affects harvest and price, while estimated price separately multiplies company and vineyard prestige ([04 modifier](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/services/wine/winescore/landValueModifierCalculation.ts#L96), [04 harvest-anchor input](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/services/wine/anchors/wineAnchorService.ts#L106), [04 price calculation](https://github.com/gram12321/winemaker04/blob/0c5f9b301457a143a4c686ba2449f215c97f1e16/src/lib/services/wine/winescore/wineScoreCalculation.ts#L32)). This is useful legacy evidence, not a 05 formula.

I inspected local Industri Clicker HEAD `9d3165a`; its prestige and quality files match the migration inventory's pinned `a6c85c0` snapshot. [PrestigeLedger](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/prestige/prestige.ts#L6) gives events a type, amount, game time, source ID and optional half-life; live balance/assets are upserted while sales, achievements and penalties are source-keyed events, and [the calculator](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/prestige/prestigeCalculator.ts#L73) derives current amounts. This helps explain attribution and time effects, but is company prestige, not vineyard reputation. Its expired-event pruning also means retained events alone cannot guarantee replay protection. [Industri quality math](https://github.com/gram12321/industriClicker/blob/a6c85c0b6d3b832c3a23829a134d049ecef01bbc/game/quality/qualityMath.ts#L76) separates weighted input quality from research, upgrade, production and staff ceilings; that decomposition is an accounting pattern, not a terroir analogue.

Recommended boundary for review: compute Terroir Potential from site, grape and growing conditions, feeding harvest/raw-material state only; do not read site price or prestige, and do not apply it again as a price multiplier after its physical effects flow into current wine assessment. Keep Origin Prestige as an origin-scoped commercial input to buyer interest or price, independent of agricultural fit and land appraisal. Appraise land as a company asset on its own rules. Attribute a sale or award to company reputation, origin reputation, or an explicit split; avoid stacking the same event as full company and origin prestige. Feed price from current wine assessment and market/buyer context, applying the origin premium once.

Focused tests should contrast high-potential/low-prestige and low-potential/high-prestige sites: harvest potential and sensory assessment follow the former, while only the latter changes the origin premium. Changing land price or area should affect asset value only; changing origin prestige should leave raw material and wine assessment unchanged. Include a price-breakdown assertion that each source contributes once, plus repeat-delivery coverage for source-keyed prestige events. These are proposed checks, not approved formulas or tests run.
