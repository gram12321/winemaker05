# Topic 10 current research: transformative fermentation and finite extraction

Approved direction is causal, time-dependent physical wine development; fermentation equations remain open and finite extraction is still a proposal ([transition overview](../../docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_Transition_Overview.md#section-4-a-genuine-wine-development-engine-fermentation-maturation-storage-and-bottle-evolution), [migration rows 138–139](../../docs/WorkingDocs/Winemaker04_to_05_System_Migration_Research.md)). The current contract assigns `developLot(lot, environment, integerDays)` one consolidated update, daily order fermentation/extraction → maturation → risk → age, and field ownership without inventory or score edits ([transition-contracts](../transition-contracts.md)).

05 already has the needed state in [types.ts](../../src/game/wine/types.ts): sugar, alcohol, yeast activity, expressed/remaining phenolics, process settings and age. [Operations.ts](../../src/game/wine/Operations.ts) depletes remaining phenolics during pressing, limits cumulative phenolics to inherited capacity, sets a 4 g/L fermentable/bottling threshold, and lets configuration restart fermentation by restoring yeast to at least 0.24. `wine/Development.ts`, `Maturation.ts` and `Risks.ts` were absent in the inspected source; the contract is not yet implemented.

In 04, `C:/GitHub/winemaker04/src/lib/services/wine/winery/fermentationManager.ts` applies weekly characteristic/anchor changes and recalculates derived indices, while `.../characteristics/fermentationCharacteristics.ts` supplies repeated fixed method/temperature bonuses; neither consumes sugar into alcohol. `.../workcalculators/crushingWorkCalculator.ts` bases work and juice yield on pressing and its completion path discards `qualityPenalty`. This supports replacing additive bonuses with material changes.

Provisional game kinetics: per day consume `min(sugar−4, 8 × clamp(yeastActivity/0.5) × temperatureFit × alcoholFit)` g/L; add `0.058` alcohol percentage points per g/L consumed. Name and tune all constants as game parameters, not wine-science claims. Let temperature fit decline with actual `environment.temperatureC` away from the chosen process temperature; let alcohol fit decline above a named stress point. Activity below 0.24 stalls with sugar remaining; `configureFermentation` restarts it. During skin contact, transfer a fixed daily fraction of remaining phenolics, capped by that remainder; stop fermentation at ≤4 g/L and set form to wine. Increment age once/day and retain all unrelated fields.

Critical scenarios: exact sugar/alcohol conservation and cap boundaries; low yeast or hostile temperature stalls without changing sugar, then restart resumes; sugar at 4 stops cleanly; zero skin-contact extracts none and repeated contact never exceeds anchor capacity. Seven daily calls must equal one seven-day call under identical conditions; an intervening temperature change should produce a different valid result, demonstrating path dependence.
