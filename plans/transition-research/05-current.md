## Recommended contract and checks

Use a pure, domain-neutral evaluator over finite axis values and configured targets. Validate finite `low`, `high`, `weight` and values, `low <= high`, and nonnegative weights. Return the normalized weighted mean of per-axis fits, bounded to `[0,1]`, with each axis carrying its target bounds, observed value, fit, weight and reasons. Keep the falloff curve explicit/configurable rather than importing 04’s midpoint formula. Build contextual targets separately with deterministic, named shift limits, axis bounds and minimum widths so an extreme value cannot move its own target until it excuses itself. Keep faults in `WineAssessment` as a separate assessment output; do not fold them into structural fit or mutate lots, finished scores or snapshots here.

Proposed focused tests (not run): deterministic results; in-range, boundary and out-of-range fit; unequal and zero weights; rejection of non-finite/reversed/negative inputs; bounded shifts and minimum width under extreme context; reason propagation; faults leave structure fit unchanged; and inputs remain unmodified.
